# Fitted exponents (fit / **predicted**)

Slopes are least-squares slopes of log cost vs log ε over the stated window. No standard errors are given: the costs are dyadic step functions with correlated residuals. Instead each fit lists its window (ε-range, decades, number of tolerances, 6 per decade) and the slopes over the upper/lower half of the window as a stability indicator. Cost = max over the three reply paths.
